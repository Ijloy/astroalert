# Typography System

> "Scientific knowledge deserves beautiful communication."

---

# Purpose

The AstroAlert Typography System defines how written information is presented throughout the platform.

Typography is not only a method of displaying text.

It creates hierarchy, emotion and understanding.

Astronomy contains information of incredible scale:

- Distances between galaxies.
- Planetary measurements.
- Scientific explanations.
- Historical discoveries.

The typography system transforms complex information into experiences that feel clear, elegant and approachable.

---

# Content

# Typography Philosophy

Cosmic Glass typography follows three fundamental principles:

## Clarity

Users should understand information quickly.

Typography must reduce cognitive effort.

---

## Hierarchy

Important information should always be immediately visible.

The interface should guide the user's attention naturally.

---

## Elegance

Scientific content deserves a beautiful presentation.

The typography should feel modern, precise and timeless.

---

# Font Direction

AstroAlert uses a combination of two typography personalities:

## Primary Typeface — Modern Sans Serif

Purpose:

Used for the majority of the interface.

Characteristics:

- Clean.
- Highly readable.
- Modern.
- Professional.
- Minimal.

Usage:

- Navigation.
- Paragraphs.
- Descriptions.
- Interface labels.
- General information.

The primary typeface represents technology and accessibility.

---

## Display Typeface — Elegant Scientific Accent

Purpose:

Used for important moments and visual experiences.

Characteristics:

- More expressive.
- Sophisticated.
- Memorable.

Usage:

- Hero titles.
- Featured discoveries.
- Major astronomical events.

The display typeface represents wonder and exploration.

---

# Typography Scale

## Display

Purpose:

Large emotional statements.

Examples:

- Universe discoveries.
- Main landing sections.
- Featured astronomical events.

Characteristics:

Large, expressive and impactful.

---

## Heading 1

Purpose:

Main page titles.

Examples:

- Planets.
- Eclipses.
- Telescope.

Characteristics:

Strong hierarchy and immediate recognition.

---

## Heading 2

Purpose:

Major sections.

Examples:

- Planet information.
- Observation details.
- Scientific explanations.

---

## Heading 3

Purpose:

Subsections and grouped information.

---

## Body Large

Purpose:

Important descriptions.

Examples:

- Educational explanations.
- Featured content.

---

## Body Regular

Purpose:

Main reading content.

Examples:

- Articles.
- Scientific information.

---

## Caption

Purpose:

Supporting information.

Examples:

- Dates.
- Sources.
- Small metadata.

---

# Astronomical Data Typography

Numbers are extremely important in astronomy.

Distances, temperatures, sizes and coordinates should receive special treatment.

Examples:

- 384,400 km
- 27.3 days
- 29.78 km/s

Data should feel precise and valuable.

Large astronomical numbers may use:

- Increased size.
- Increased spacing.
- Strong contrast.

The goal is to make scientific information understandable.

---

# Text Hierarchy Rules

## Rule 1

Important information must be visible before secondary information.

---

## Rule 2

Avoid excessive text density.

The universe is complex.

The interface should not be.

---

## Rule 3

Titles should create curiosity.

Not only describe.

Example:

Less effective:

"Moon Information"

Better:

"Earth's Silent Companion"

The interface should invite exploration.

---

## Rule 4

Scientific accuracy must remain clear.

Creative language should never replace correct information.

---

# Tone Through Typography

Typography should communicate:

## Intelligence

Through precision and organization.

## Calm

Through spacing and readability.

## Wonder

Through carefully selected moments of emphasis.

## Trust

Through consistency and clarity.

---

# Typography in Components

## Buttons

Should be:

- Clear.
- Confident.
- Short.

---

## Cards

Should prioritize:

1. Title.
2. Main information.
3. Supporting details.

---

## Data Displays

Should prioritize:

1. Value.
2. Unit.
3. Context.

---

## Navigation

Should remain subtle.

Navigation helps exploration but should never dominate.

---

# Design Implications

Typography affects every part of AstroAlert:

- Page hierarchy.
- Cards.

- Astronomy data.
- Educational content.
- Animations.
- Accessibility.

Every text element must have a clear purpose.
