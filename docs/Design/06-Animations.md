# Motion System

> "Motion exists to guide, never to distract."

---

# Purpose

The AstroAlert Motion System defines how animation and movement are used throughout the platform.

Motion is not decoration.

It is a communication tool.

Animations help users understand:

- Relationships between elements.
- Changes in information.
- Navigation between experiences.
- Important moments.

The purpose of motion in AstroAlert is to create a feeling of exploration while maintaining clarity and elegance.

---

# Content

# Motion Philosophy

The universe is constantly moving.

Planets orbit.

Stars evolve.

Galaxies travel through space.

However, cosmic movement is not chaotic.

It follows patterns.

AstroAlert follows the same principle.

Every animation must feel:

- Natural.
- Smooth.
- Meaningful.
- Controlled.

---

# Core Motion Principles

## Purpose Before Animation

Every movement must answer:

"Why does this move?"

If the answer is only:

"Because it looks cool."

The animation should not exist.

---

## Subtle Over Spectacular

AstroAlert should create wonder through information and discovery.

Not through excessive effects.

Animations should enhance the experience without becoming the experience.

---

## Consistency Creates Familiarity

Similar interactions should always behave similarly.

Users should learn how AstroAlert moves.

---

# Motion Categories

## Navigation Motion

Purpose:

Help users understand transitions between sections.

Examples:

- Moving between Planets and Eclipses.
- Opening exploration areas.
- Changing views.

Characteristics:

- Smooth.
- Predictable.
- Fast enough to maintain flow.

---

## Discovery Motion

Purpose:

Highlight important astronomical information.

Examples:

- A new discovery appearing.
- A celestial event being revealed.
- Important information becoming visible.

Characteristics:

- Creates curiosity.
- Feels intentional.
- Does not interrupt.

---

## Data Motion

Purpose:

Represent changing astronomical information.

Examples:

- ISS movement.
- Planet positions.
- Real-time data updates.

Characteristics:

- Accurate.
- Calm.
- Informative.

---

## Interaction Motion

Purpose:

Provide feedback to user actions.

Examples:

- Button states.
- Selecting objects.
- Opening cards.

Characteristics:

- Immediate.
- Clear.
- Responsive.

---

# Timing Principles

Animations should feel natural.

Fast animations communicate:

- Immediate action.
- Simple feedback.

Medium animations communicate:

- Navigation.
- State changes.

Slow animations communicate:

- Discovery.
- Important moments.

Slow does not mean better.

The experience should always respect the user's time.

---

# Animation Rules

## Rule 1

Never animate everything.

Movement requires contrast.

---

## Rule 2

Important information should appear clearly before being beautifully animated.

---

## Rule 3

Animations should support understanding.

---

## Rule 4

Avoid unnecessary continuous movement.

The interface should remain calm.

---

# Accessibility

Motion should respect different user preferences.

The experience should support users who prefer reduced movement.

Important information should never depend only on animation.

---

# Motion Examples

## Planet Exploration

A planet may slowly appear with depth and lighting.

The purpose is to create a sense of discovery.

---

## ISS Tracking

The movement of the station should represent real orbital motion.

The animation should communicate location, not decoration.

---

## Eclipse Experience

The transition between celestial bodies should explain alignment.

The movement teaches the concept.
